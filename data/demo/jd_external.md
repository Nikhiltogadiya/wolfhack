# IT Infrastructure & Information Security Manager

Meridian Logistics Group — Berlin (hybrid, 3 days on site)

We are hiring an IT Infrastructure & Information Security Manager to own the systems that keep
a 900-person logistics business running, and to lead the small team that looks after them.

## What you'll do
Own our enterprise infrastructure end to end — Active Directory, network, storage and the
server estate. Lead a team of four engineers. Run our information-security programme, including
risk assessment, audit response and security awareness. Plan and deliver hardware and software
upgrades across the estate. Act as escalation point for major incidents.

## Requirements
- At least 5 years of enterprise IT infrastructure experience
- Demonstrated experience managing or leading a technical team
- Hands-on Active Directory administration and design
- Network administration experience (routing, switching, firewalls)
- Experience with information security risk management and audit
- Experience planning and executing infrastructure upgrade projects
- Bachelor's degree in a technical field, or equivalent demonstrable experience
- Must have the right to work in Germany

## Preferred
- Security certification such as CISSP, CISM or Security+ is desirable
- Experience in logistics, transport or manufacturing is a plus
- Familiarity with virtualisation (VMware or Hyper-V) preferred
- Disaster recovery and business continuity planning would be great
- Ideally some exposure to cloud migration (Azure or AWS)
- German language skills are advantageous but not required
