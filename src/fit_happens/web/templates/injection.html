How it works

Two independent detectors, because they fail in different places. A vendored detector from a USENIX Security 2026 study inspects every text span for sub-point fonts, fill colour matching the background, flat visual regions and phantom ink. Cross-engine divergence catches what that cannot see: text placed outside the page box is extracted by pdfplumber and pdfminer but not by pymupdf or pypdfium2 — so if two independent parsers disagree about what a file says, something is hidden in it. That generalises to hiding techniques nobody has invented yet.
